Our Services

Outdoor services for homes, second homes and commercial properties throughout Park City, Heber Valley and the Wasatch Back.

Landscaping

We design, improve and maintain landscapes that hold up in Park City's mountain climate and suit the property they belong to.

Includes: landscape installation, renovations, decorative rock and mulch, seasonal plantings.

Lawn Mowing

Recurring mowing, trimming, edging and cleanup through the growing season, on a reliable schedule.

Includes: weekly mowing, edging, detail trimming, blowing and cleanup.

Property Maintenance

Maintenance plans for primary residences, vacation homes and commercial properties, built around what each property needs.

Includes: landscape maintenance, lawn care, spring and fall cleanup, vacation home care.

Snow Removal

Driveways, walkways and entrances kept clear through the winter. Best arranged before the first big storm.

Includes: residential and commercial snow removal, vacation home service, ice management.

Dependable Strength. Trustworthy Results.

Owning property in Park City means a short growing season, high elevation, irrigation demands and long winters. Oxen brings local experience to those conditions instead of treating every property the same.

We handle lawn care and landscaping in summer and snow removal in winter, so you work with one local company all year. That matters most for second homes, where the owner isn't always there to keep an eye on things.

Questions

What services does Oxen provide?

Yes. Recurring lawn mowing and lawn maintenance are available for properties within our lawn care service area.

Yes. Recurring property care is especially useful for owners who need dependable local maintenance while they're away.
